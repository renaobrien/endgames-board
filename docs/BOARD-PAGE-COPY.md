# endgam.es/board page copy

Copy for the `/board` page on the website. Layout and styling follow the site's v2 design.

## Hero

**Headline:** Your set. On your table.

**Subhead:** A touchscreen chess board you build at home. It plays your live Endgames games with your custom pieces, and stays in sync with the website.

**Buttons:** "Build one" (goes to the build guide) and "Pair your board" (goes to /board/pair)

## How it works

1. **Get the board.** One off-the-shelf 5" touchscreen, about $43. No soldering, no wiring.
2. **Print the case.** Two parts, no supports, four screws.
3. **Pair it.** The board shows a code. Type it at endgam.es/board/pair.
4. **Play.** Tap a piece, tap where it goes. Your opponent can be on the website, the app, or their own board.

## What you need

- Elecrow CrowPanel Advanced 5" ESP32-P4 touchscreen
- A 3D printer and some PLA
- 4 x M2.5 x 20 mm screws
- A USB-C cable and power adapter

## Open source

Everything you need to build one is free on GitHub: the case files, the firmware, and the guide. Build it, change it, share your version.

**Link:** "See the build guide on GitHub"
